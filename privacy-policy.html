Privacy Policy for Simply Legendary

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Simply Legendary (Android package `simply.studios`) is a single-player game published by Simply Studios. This policy describes what information the game uses.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your device, including gold, gems, unlocks, cosmetics, settings, and any items you have bought. That data stays in the app's local storage under the key `simplyLegendary`. We do not operate a game server, and we do not require an account.

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up that local save to your Google account so it can be restored after a reinstall. That backup is handled by Google, not by a Simply Studios server.

Purchases

If you buy Gems, a cosmetic bundle, the Founder's Pack, or Remove Ads, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details. We do not receive or store your credit card number. The game records only what you already own (for example, that the Founder's Pack or Remove Ads has been granted) in the local save so the items can be restored.

You can restore non-consumable purchases (the Founder's Pack, Remove Ads, and cosmetic bundles) with the Restore Purchases button in the Store.

This build does not send receipts to a Simply Studios server for validation.

Advertising (AdMob)

The Android app uses Google AdMob to show ads. You may choose to watch a rewarded ad to revive once during a run or to double the gold from a finished run. A short full-screen interstitial may appear after some runs. Buying Remove Ads, or the Founder's Pack, turns those interstitials off. Rewarded ads stay optional.

Before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form when it is required (including under the GDPR in the EU/UK and under applicable US state privacy laws). You can change your advertising choices later from Settings → Ad privacy in the Android app.

AdMob (Google) may collect and process:

  • Device or other identifiers, including the advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (not precise GPS)
  • App interactions, such as ad impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostic or performance information related to ads

That information is sent to Google over an encrypted connection and is used to provide and measure advertising. We do not receive a copy of it on a Simply Studios server.

You can also reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings (for example, Ads or Privacy settings), and you can learn more in Google's advertising privacy policy.

The desktop and browser builds of the game do not show ads and do not load the AdMob SDK.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location. The game has no separate analytics, crash, or social SDK of our own.

The app uses the Internet permission so that Google Play can complete purchases and so that AdMob can show ads on Android. The game itself can be played offline, but ads and purchases need a network connection.

Children

The game may be suitable for a range of ages once it has a content rating, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The AdMob consent form is tagged as not for users under the age of consent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at the same privacy policy URL listed on the Play Store listing.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer email listed on the Simply Legendary Play Store page.
